# Share a Campaign Story

The **Campaign Story** page (**Reports → Campaign Story**) generates a narrative summary of everything that happened during a campaign: deployments, lab results, annotations, and data freshness.

## Generate a story

1. Go to **Reports → Campaign Story**.
2. Select a campaign from the dropdown.
3. The page builds a timeline of equipment, sampling locations, lab panels, and annotations.

## What to share

- Copy the timeline or KPI cards into a report.
- Use the data freshness section to confirm which streams are up to date.
- Export plots from **Operations → Visualize Data** and include them alongside the story.
